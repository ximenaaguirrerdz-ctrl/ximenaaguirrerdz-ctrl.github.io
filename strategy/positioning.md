# Positioning system

## Primary positioning

Senior B2B Marketing & Communications leader who turns complex products and business priorities into stories people understand, moments they act on and systems that create measurable market and pipeline impact.

## Portfolio thesis

> I find the story. Then I build the system that makes it travel.

## Recruiter takeaway

Ximena knows how to find the market tension, turn it into a communications and GTM strategy, bring people around it, execute across channels and markets, and show what it did for the business. She can lead the work—and still do the work.

## Role lanes

1. Senior / Lead / Manager — Integrated B2B Marketing & Communications
2. Senior Communications / External Communications / Corporate Communications
3. Field Marketing / Regional Marketing
4. Selective Product and GTM Communications

## Deliberate exclusions

The portfolio does not position Ximena as a pure growth marketer, social-first creator, paid-media specialist, developer or machine-learning engineer.
