# Positioning and copy system

## Primary positioning

Madrid-based Senior Communications & Marketing Manager with 10 years across corporate and internal communications, PR, Product Marketing, GTM and Field Marketing for complex B2B technology and fintech across Europe and Latin America.

## Human thesis

Ximena works where a strong product, smart people and active teams still need a shared version of what matters. She gets close to the product, asks the question others are skipping, finds language people can use and stays close enough to the work to learn what happened next.

## Search layer

Titles, descriptions, headings, semantic links and structured data use the exact language recruiters search for. Body copy uses those terms only where they sound natural and can be substantiated.

## Voice rules

- Use a complete thought when the nuance matters; not every section needs a slogan.
- Let specific nouns, verbs and evidence carry the copy instead of metaphors.
- Reserve first person for judgment, contribution and experience—not every headline.
- Keep exact recruiter keywords in metadata, factual labels, the résumé and recruiter view.
- Vary sentence length without relying on repeated contrast formulas.
- Use questions only when they reproduce a real business conversation.
- Avoid agency-coded language such as “brief” when the work is an in-house business decision.
- Attribute team and programme results fairly.
- Never manufacture reach, precision or causality.

The benchmark blend is: Netflix-style directness and memorable tension; GoCardless-style problem-to-benefit clarity; Trade Republic-style plain verbs, short claims and immediate proof. The result should sound like Ximena, not like any of those brands.

## Anchor lines

- “That’s usually where I come in.”
- “My job is to find the version they can all move with.”
- “I kept getting closer to the decision.”
- “I like getting close to the thing.”
- “I like knowing what happened next.”
- “Some of my best writing has someone else’s name on it.”
- “I disappear into the voice, not the thinking.”

## Deliberate exclusions

Do not position Ximena as a pure growth marketer, social-first creator, paid-media specialist, Product Manager, software developer, data scientist or machine-learning engineer. Do not put those negative disclaimers in recruiter-facing copy; show the credible lane clearly instead.
