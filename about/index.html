The honest version

I like getting close to the thing.

The shortest explanation of my career is that I kept moving closer to the source of the decision.

I began in roles where communications had to earn attention and a response. Public affairs, fashion and social impact taught me that context changes the meaning of a message. Regional PR taught me that translation is not relevance. Internal communications taught me that employees cannot be treated as an afterthought.

Product Marketing, Field Marketing and lifecycle work moved me closer to Product, Sales and measurement. My remit expanded from shaping the message to making choices about audience, positioning, launch strategy, handoffs and evidence.

I like working at both levels: the decision itself and the detailed work needed to make it hold. I will ask Product to explain the claim again, help a leader find the sentence they actually mean, write the brief, sit with Sales after the event and question a report whose conclusion looks cleaner than its evidence.

What stays constant

Ask one more question.

The overlooked question often contains the audience insight or the real decision.

Understand before simplifying.

Especially when the subject is regulated, technical or depends on trust.

Stay accountable for the recommendation.

I care whether it holds up with Product, Sales, leadership and the market.
