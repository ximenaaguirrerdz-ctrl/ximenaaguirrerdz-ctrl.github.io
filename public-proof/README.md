# Public Work & Evidence Repository

This folder is a public evidence layer for Ximena Aguirre's work across marketing, communications, PR, executive visibility, product and GTM communications, field marketing, events, customer storytelling, research-led campaigns and freelance projects.

## Scope

The master inventory is maintained in English and organized from newest to oldest. It distinguishes:

- **Employment** — public work produced during an employed role.
- **Freelance** — client/project work, including Thinky, InstaCrops and Wizz.
- **Earned media** — interviews, profiles, bylines, research coverage and customer/company stories.
- **Product & GTM** — launches, partnerships, market education and customer proof.
- **Field marketing & events** — owned events, sponsorships, executive programs and ecosystem activity.
- **Owned content** — signed articles, customer stories, podcasts and campaign assets.
- **Offline/private evidence** — sanitized records created when the original proof exists in private reports, email threads, scans or clippings that should not expose personal information.

## Current master inventory

The consolidated tracker currently contains **110 evidence items**, including **104 items with public URLs**. The largest bodies of proof cover Belvo, 100 Ladrillos, Zendesk, Expok/client work, Thinky, WeWork, Wizz, InstaCrops and COPRED.

## Evidence principles

1. Keep full public URLs whenever they exist.
2. Preserve the original outlet/platform and publication date where verifiable.
3. Separate employment work from freelance work.
4. Do not claim pre-tenure or post-tenure coverage as direct work unless evidence supports attribution.
5. Do not publish private email addresses, internal correspondence or confidential material merely to create a portfolio link.
6. Use sanitized evidence pages for offline/private proof.
