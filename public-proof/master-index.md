## Evidence and attribution rules

- Public URLs are preserved in full wherever recovered.
- Belvo articles explicitly attributed to Ximena Aguirre by Belvo's official author archive are labelled **direct authorship confirmed**.
- Earned-media links document the communications programme, media footprint or client/account visibility; they are **not automatically treated as individual authorship**.
- Thinky, Wizz and InstaCrops are explicitly labelled **Freelance**.
- Pre-tenure and post-tenure references are retained as **context**, not silently converted into personal results.
- Unresolved historical references are kept instead of being discarded.
- Offline/private proof should be represented only through sanitized evidence records; raw private emails, tracking links and personal contact data are not published.
- The spreadsheet master is the canonical row-level archive because it preserves date precision, duplicate pickups, verification status, campaign/workstream and attribution notes that are too granular for these GitHub summary pages.
