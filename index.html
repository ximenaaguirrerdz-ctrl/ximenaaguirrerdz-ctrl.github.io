02 / THE LONG GAME

I trust relationships that outlast the first campaign.

I want to understand who the buyer is, what Sales hears, what clients need and which relationships already matter. A customer doesn't become a champion because we ask for a quote; that kind of trust grows from listening, making useful work together and staying close.

I enjoy building those connections—not only with media, but also with customers, experts and partners who can introduce the business to new people over time.
