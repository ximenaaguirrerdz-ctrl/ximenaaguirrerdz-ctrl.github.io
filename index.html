How I became 360°

I kept following the same story.

I did not build my range by collecting channels. I built it by asking what a story needs next.

Sometimes it needs evidence. Sometimes a leader who can say it plainly. Sometimes employees need to hear it first. And sometimes the story needs a room, a follow-up journey and a commercial owner before it can do anything useful.

  1. 01 / ScrutinyCSR taught me to prove the claim.

    I learned to look for the person, place and consequence behind a corporate statement.

  2. 02 / RelevanceRegional PR taught me to make it local.

    At Zendesk, I carried one thesis across six markets and the Caribbean without flattening what made each market care.

  3. 03 / AlignmentInternal comms taught me to start inside.

    At WeWork, I built a clearer rhythm for 2,000+ colleagues because employees should not discover the company story last.

  4. 04 / MovementField and CRM taught me where attention goes next.

    At 100 Ladrillos and Belvo, I connected PR, content, live moments, Sales follow-up and measurable demand.
